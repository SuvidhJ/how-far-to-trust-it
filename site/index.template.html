Customer support on Twitter · evaluation first

A support agent for , and how far to trust it

It reads a customer's tweet, classifies what they want, drafts a reply from how the brand answered similar messages before, and then either sends the reply itself or hands it to a person with a stated reason. Most of the work went into the proof: hand-labelled data, two baselines, an independent LLM annotator, and a list of what the numbers do not show.
