## 3. Read by hand, and what changed (2026-09-18)

The flag column passes all 4 auto-handled drafts, but 2 of them state policy as fact:
the bereavement answer and the Executive Platinum upgrade claim. The regexes look for
promised money and claimed actions, not policy claims. After this run `ask_policy`
became a never-auto-handled intent (DECISIONS #53). Replayed offline with that rule
(`--offline`, same drafts), the bereavement message escalates and 3 of 14 are
auto-handled. The loyalty-status policy claim is still auto-sent (REPORT §3.6).
Sections 1-2 above are the live run from before the change and are kept as run.
